### D2-Q001
- question: What is the correct order of the energy hierarchy?
- A: Renewables → efficiency → reduce demand
- B: Reduce demand → system efficiency → renewables
- C: Efficiency → renewables → reduce demand
- D: Renewables → reduce demand → efficiency
- reason: Reduce demand first, then raise system efficiency, then cover the remainder with renewables.

### D2-Q002
- question: What rule opens Section 2.1?
- A: The cheapest energy is the energy we do not use
- B: The cheapest energy is solar
- C: The cheapest energy is gas
- D: The cheapest energy is night-time energy
- reason: Energy not consumed needs no source, installation or maintenance.

### D2-Q003
- question: Which measure belongs to level 1 (reduce demand)?
- A: Replacing air conditioners
- B: Installing solar panels
- C: Orientation, shading, insulation and airtightness
- D: Solar water heating
- reason: Level 1 stops heat entering or electricity being used in the first place.

### D2-Q010

- A: m²·°C/W
- B: kWh
- C: W/(m²·°C)
- D: kgCO₂e
- reason: U is heat transferred per square metre per degree of difference.

### D2-Q011
- question: The lower the U-factor:
- A: The more heat is transferred
- B: The less heat is transferred
- C: The more light enters
- D: No effect
- reason: A low U means better insulation.

### D2-Q012

- C: 25% of the glass lets light through

### D2-Q014

- A: Q = SHGC × A

- C: Q = P × PR

### D2-Q015

- A: Compressor capacity
- B: Operating hours
- C: Heat removed divided by electricity input
- D: Light-to-heat ratio
- reason: The higher the COP, the less electricity per kW of cooling.
