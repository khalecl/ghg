### D1-Q001
- question: What is the largest electricity load in most Saudi buildings?
- A: Lighting
- B: Cooling
- C: Elevators
- D: Water heating
- reason: Cooling is the largest load because of the long summer, high peak temperatures and many hours that need cooling.

### D1-Q002
- question: What do Cooling Degree Days (CDD) measure?
- A: The number of summer holidays
- B: Air-conditioner capacity in tons
- C: How much heat the building needs to remove over the year
- D: Daily AC operating hours
- reason: CDD sums the difference between the daily mean temperature and a base, so it expresses the expected annual cooling load.
